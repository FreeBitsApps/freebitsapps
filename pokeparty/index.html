PokeParty

Rankings, IV checks and a local battle simulator for Pokémon GO PvP.

Coming soon to the App Store

What it does

PokeParty is a Mac-first companion for Pokémon GO PvP, also available for iPhone and iPad. Browse the rankings, check your IVs, simulate battles on your own device, and build teams of three, all without waiting on a website.

What you get

  • Rankings. Browse Great, Ultra and Master League plus any active limited cups. Each Pokémon shows its stats, recommended moveset, key wins and counters.
  • Local battle simulator. Run any Pokémon against its entire league meta at 0, 1 or 2 shields per side, with the moveset and IVs you choose. It runs on your device.
  • IV Rank Checker. Enter a Pokémon's IVs to see its rank and stat product across Little, Great, Ultra and Master League, for its whole evolution family and at level caps of 40, 41, 50 or 51.
  • Team Builder. Grade a team of three on coverage, bulk, safety and consistency, see its biggest threats, and get teammate suggestions.
  • My Bench. Keep the Pokémon you actually own, with their IVs, and sync your bench between devices with iCloud.
  • Breakpoints and Opponent Check. See how your IV rank holds up against the top meta, and check whether your team beats a specific opponent.
  • Works offline. Game data and rankings are cached on your device and bundled for first launch, so the app is useful without a connection.

PokeParty Pro

The rankings, simulator, IV checker, Team Builder and bench are free. A one-time purchase, with no subscription, unlocks the advanced tools:

  • Party Finder. Find the best team from your bench or from the PvP meta.
  • Screen Scanner (Mac). Read a Pokémon's details from iPhone Mirroring and add it to your bench automatically.
  • Opponent scan. Detect your opponent from the Pokémon GO battle screen.
  • Themes. Recolor the app with a palette of your choice.

Before you start

PokeParty runs on Mac, iPhone and iPad. Screen Scanner is Mac only and needs Screen Recording permission, which the app asks for the first time you use it. An internet connection is used to download and refresh game data.
