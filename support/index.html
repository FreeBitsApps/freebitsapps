Support

Need a hand with one of our apps? Start with the answers below, or report a problem.

Report a problem

Open an issue on GitHub and tell us which app you're using, your device model and OS version, and what happened. A free GitHub account is needed. Issues are public, so please don't include personal information.

Crankle questions

Crankle can't search or play music

Crankle needs access to Apple Music and an Apple Music subscription. Open Crankle's Settings and tap Allow Access under Apple Music. If you previously said no, open the iPhone Settings app, choose Crankle, and turn on Media & Apple Music.

My rankings aren't syncing

Make sure you're signed in to iCloud on each device, then open Crankle's Settings and tap Sync Now under Sharing & Sync. The same section shows the current iCloud status and any error.

How do I share a ranking?

Open the ranking and use its share option to invite someone with their Apple Account. They'll get an invitation they can accept in Crankle.

How do I restore my theme purchase?

Open Crankle's Settings and tap Restore Purchases. You need to be signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.

New songs are landing in the wrong place

In Crankle's Settings, under Ranking, choose whether songs are added one at a time as they play or all at once, and whether new songs go to the top, middle or bottom of the list. In tier lists, new songs start in the unplaced pool.

How do I delete my Crankle data?

Deleting the app removes the rankings stored on your iPhone. To remove synced copies, manage Crankle's data in the iPhone Settings app under your name, then iCloud. See the privacy policy for more.

PokeParty questions

Rankings or Pokémon data won't load

PokeParty downloads game data and rankings from PvPoke the first time it runs and whenever they change. Check your internet connection, then open PokeParty's Settings and use the refresh button. Once data has loaded it is cached, so the app keeps working offline.

Why do my rankings differ slightly from the PvPoke website?

PokeParty runs its own port of PvPoke's battle engine on your device, so results are very close but not always identical. Rankings also change when PvPoke publishes new data, and PokeParty picks that up on its next refresh.

Party Finder or Screen Scanner is locked

Those tools are part of PokeParty Pro, a one-time purchase. If you already bought it, tap Restore Purchases on the Pro screen or in Settings. You need to be signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.

Screen Scanner can't see my iPhone

Screen Scanner works on the Mac with iPhone Mirroring open. Allow PokeParty under System Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Screen Recording, and restart the app. Keep the Pokémon's detail screen visible in the mirrored window.

My bench isn't syncing between devices

Make sure you're signed in to iCloud on each device and that iCloud is turned on for PokeParty. Syncing can take a short while to appear on the other device.

How do I delete my PokeParty data?

Deleting the app removes the data stored on that device. To remove synced copies, manage PokeParty's data in System Settings or the iPhone Settings app under your name, then iCloud. See the privacy policy for more.
